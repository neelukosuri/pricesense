How it works

Install once, then keep shopping the way you already do.

Open any product

Browse Amazon.in or Flipkart as usual. The PriceSense panel opens beside the product.

See the comparison

PriceSense finds the same item on the other store, checks its specifications and compares the prices and price history.

Buy at the real lowest

Click through to the cheaper listing — or set a target price and wait for the drop.

Questions

Is PriceSense free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and nothing to pay.

How does PriceSense make money?

When PriceSense finds a cheaper price on the other store and you click through to it from the panel, that link may include the developer’s affiliate tag. If you then buy something, the developer may earn a small commission from the store, at no extra cost to you. Only links to a cheaper price on the other store are ever tagged, and PriceSense never changes the page you are browsing or redirects you automatically.

What happens to my data?

Price history, your watchlist and settings are stored only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to the developer. Read the privacy policy.

Do I need to share my Amazon or Flipkart password?

Never. You sign in on the official Amazon and Flipkart pages as you normally do; PriceSense never sees or stores your password.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on desktop. The extension is launching soon on the Chrome Web Store and Edge Add-ons.

Is PriceSense part of Amazon or Flipkart?

No. PriceSense is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon or Flipkart.
